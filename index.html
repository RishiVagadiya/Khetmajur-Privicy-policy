Lawful Grounds for Data Processing (DPDP Act 2023)

In accordance with Sections 4 and 6 of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, data is processed solely on the basis of explicit, informed user consent for the following services:

  • Local Agricultural Employment: Connecting farmers with skilled workers in their district and helping laborers find timely farm jobs nearby.
  • Live Mandi Commodity Rates: Providing transparent, up-to-date commodity market prices across major APMC yards in Gujarat.
  • Instant Push Notifications: Delivering alerts to nearby laborers when a farmer posts a new agricultural work requirement.
3

Location Data & Google Firebase Integration

📍 Location Privacy Commitment:

We do not track or record your real-time background GPS coordinates. The application only utilizes the District and Village/Taluka selected by you to filter available farm labor and display regional APMC prices.

☁️ Google Firebase Security Architecture:

KhetMajur integrates Google Firebase services (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore Database, and Firebase Cloud Messaging). All data in transit and at rest is protected using industry-standard TLS encryption and AES-256 cloud security protocols.

4

Google AdMob Monetization & Ad Policy

The KhetMajur platform is 100% free for both farmers and laborers. To sustain infrastructure costs, server maintenance, and SMS gateway charges, the app displays banner and interstitial advertisements served via the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob).

  • Google AdMob delivers non-sensitive, family-safe advertisements in compliance with Google Developer Policies.
  • We do not share any personal identity information, phone numbers, or private user data with advertisers.
  • Users can adjust or turn off personalized advertising at any time through their personal Google Account Ad Preferences.
