Lawful Grounds for Data Processing (DPDP Act 2023)

In accordance with Sections 4 and 6 of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, data is processed solely on the basis of explicit, informed user consent for the following services:

  • Local Agricultural Employment: Connecting farmers with skilled workers in their district and helping laborers find timely farm jobs nearby.
  • Live Mandi Commodity Rates: Providing transparent, up-to-date commodity market prices across major APMC yards in Gujarat.
  • Instant Push Notifications: Delivering alerts to nearby laborers when a farmer posts a new agricultural work requirement.
  • Performance Monitoring & Analytics: Tracking application reliability, diagnosing crashes, evaluating engagement trends, and verifying test releases using Google Firebase Analytics.
3

Location Data, Google Firebase & Analytics Integration

📍 Location Privacy Commitment:

We do not track or record your real-time background GPS coordinates. The application only utilizes the District and Village/Taluka selected by you to filter available farm labor and display regional APMC prices.

☁️ Google Firebase Security Architecture:

KhetMajur integrates Google Firebase services (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore Database, and Firebase Cloud Messaging). All data in transit and at rest is protected using industry-standard TLS encryption and AES-256 cloud security protocols.

📊 Google Firebase Analytics & Diagnostics:

We utilize Google Firebase Analytics (provided by Google LLC) to analyze how users interact with the KhetMajur application and maintain high stability across various Android devices:

  • Metrics Tracked: Screen navigation flow (via Firebase Analytics Observer), session frequency, daily active usage, app open timestamps, feature interactions (e.g., job postings, job applications, mandi rate lookups), role classification (Farmer or Agricultural Worker), and crash/stability logs.
  • Device Diagnostics: Anonymized device/tester identifier, operating system version, device manufacturer, and Firebase installation ID.
  • Strict Non-Commercial Use: We strictly do not sell, rent, or trade your analytics data to third-party data brokers or marketing firms. Data is analyzed strictly in aggregate to enhance app functionality and user experience.
  • Privacy & Security Standards: All analytics telemetry is transmitted via secure TLS encryption. Data processing conforms to Google's privacy protocols and India's DPDP Act, 2023.
  • Third-Party Policy Reference: To learn more about how Google safeguards analytics telemetry, please refer to the Google Privacy Policy and Firebase Data & Privacy Guidelines.
